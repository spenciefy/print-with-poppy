## Design Context
Users are people asking personal agents to print meaningful photos, plus developers exploring Poppy commerce. The job is simple home-delivered photo prints, with clear approval and order visibility.
Brand: warm, playful, tactile. User supplied an orange poppy flower and requested poppy orange. “Print out your memories” leads; protocol details stay in the developer guide.
Light paper surfaces, deep orange ink, scattered photographic prints, generous editorial spacing. Young Serif evokes a printed keepsake; Hanken Grotesk supplies quiet functional text. Rejected reflex fonts: Fraunces, Instrument Serif, DM Sans. Font sources reviewed in Google Fonts CSS.
Principles: make the memories the hero; one clear action; show real delivery/payment state; complete address and crop preview before approval; keyboard-accessible, responsive, reduced-motion-aware UI.
