## Design Context

Users are people asking personal agents to print meaningful photos, plus developers exploring Poppy commerce. The job is simple home-delivered photo prints, with clear approval and order visibility.
Brand: warm, playful, tactile. User supplied an orange poppy flower and requested poppy orange. “Print out your memories” leads; protocol details stay in the developer guide.
Light paper surfaces, deep orange ink, scattered photographic prints, generous editorial spacing. Young Serif evokes a printed keepsake; Hanken Grotesk supplies quiet functional text. Rejected reflex fonts: Fraunces, Instrument Serif, DM Sans. Font sources reviewed in Google Fonts CSS.
Principles: make the memories the hero; one clear action; show real delivery/payment state; complete address and crop preview before approval; keyboard-accessible, responsive, reduced-motion-aware UI.

Latest direction: restrained, clear, personal. Exactly two homepage sections; no stock photos, photo mockups, ornamental slogans or promotional filler. Keep the orange poppy mark, existing typefaces, one agent copy action, and brief factual ordering details.

Homepage correction: no hero layout or oversized display headline. Use a compact page with a visible square instruction sheet and an inline Copy button. Keep the entire prompt selectable; do not hide it in disclosure.

Latest creator direction: a minimal agent-only store with two clear product offers and pricing, a standard copy icon, and a footer FAQ explaining actual Poppy discovery/authentication/API/approval support. No manual shopping CTA. Keep the abstract square orange poppy logo and honest payment availability.

Current direction: single-column storefront at every size; concise product rows with a small example image per SKU. Logo is a layered stack of the existing poppy instant-photo mark, with restrained playful entrance and hover motion, respecting reduced motion.
